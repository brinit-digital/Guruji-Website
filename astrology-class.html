Our Astrology Class

Astrology is the study of the movements and positions of celestial bodies—like the Sun, Moon, planets, and stars—and how they are believed to influence human life and natural events on Earth.

In simple terms: Astrology is the belief system that says where the planets and stars were at the time of your birth can affect your personality, relationships, career, and even fate.

Example:

If someone is born on August 15, astrology might say:

Their Sun sign is Leo, which means they may be confident, creative, and strong-willed. Depending on where other planets were, their emotional behavior, career strengths, and relationship patterns could also be interpreted. Key Concepts in Astrology

1. Zodiac Signs – The 12 divisions of the sky that the Sun moves through in a year (Aries, Taurus, Gemini, etc.)
2. Horoscope – A chart or prediction based on your birth time and date
3. Natal (Birth) Chart – A map of the sky when you were born; used to understand your traits and life path.
4. Houses – 12 parts of the sky that represent areas of life (career, family, love, etc.)
5. Planets – Each planet represents different energies (e.g., Venus = love, Mars = action).
6. Aspects – Angles between planets; they show how planets interact and affect you.

Types of Astrology

Western Astrology – Based on the tropical zodiac (mostly used in Europe/America)
Vedic Astrology (Jyotisha) – Ancient Indian system based on the sidereal zodiac
Chinese Astrology – Based on birth years and lunar cycles with animal signs
Mayan or Egyptian Astrology – Other ancient systems with different foundations

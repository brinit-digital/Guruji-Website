Our Vastu Class

Vastu (or Vastu Shastra) is the ancient Indian science of architecture and space design, which guides how buildings, homes, and rooms should be constructed and arranged to create harmony with nature and cosmic energies.

Vastu Shastra Topics in Classes

Level 1: Introduction to Vastu (Basic Foundation) - Beginners Level
1. What is Vastu Shastra? – History & Importance
2. Scientific & Spiritual Basis of Vastu
3. The Panchabhutas (Five Elements) – Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space
4. The 8 Cardinal Directions – Energies and ruling deities
5. Understanding Vastu Purusha Mandala
6. Types of Lands and Their Energies
7. Auspicious Time & Site Selection (Bhoomi Vichar)
8. Basics of Compass Reading & Direction Analysis
9. Introduction to Floor Plan and Space Division
Level 2: Residential Vastu (Home Vastu) - Intermediate level
1. Ideal House Orientation
2. Main Entrance Vastu
3. Room-wise Vastu:

Pooja Room, Kitchen, Bedroom, Bathroom/Toilets, Living/Dining Room, Study Room, Staircase & Balcony

4. Vastu for Apartments vs Independent Houses
5. Vastu for Compound Wall, Gate & Garden
6. Interior Arrangements & Furniture Placement
7. Color Psychology in Vastu
8. Vastu Remedies without Demolition
Level 3: Commercial and Industrial Vastu - For business professionals
1. Vastu for Shops & Offices
2. Factory and Industrial Layout Planning
3. Showrooms, Malls, Hotels, Hospitals, Schools
4. Vastu for Banks, Temples, and Government Buildings
5. Vastu for Corporate Success – CEO Cabin, Staff Areas
6. Cash Flow, Growth & Productivity Vastu
Level 4: Advanced Vastu Analysis and Rectification - For serious learners and professionals
1. Vastu & Astrology (Astro-Vastu Integration)
2. Vastu and Numerology Matching
3. Energy Mapping & Energy Flow Analysis
4. Geopathic Stress & Earth Energy Corrections
5. Advanced Remedies using:

Pyramids, Mirrors ,Crystals, Yantras

6. Common Vastu Doshas and their Solutions
7. Vastu Case Studies and Real-life Projects
Level 5: Vastu Field Practice & Consultation Skills
1. How to do a Vastu Site Visit
2. Use of Compass, Lecher Antenna, Energy Scanners
3. Drawing and Analyzing Vastu Charts
4. Client Consultation Techniques
5. Report Writing and Vastu Corrections Plan
6. Ethics & Professionalism in Vastu Consultation
Optional Modules:
Temple Architecture (Agama Vastu)
Vastu for Interior Designers and Architects
Ancient Tamil Vastu Principles (Agathiyar, Mayan traditions)
